Connecting NYC's startup ecosystem with NYC's developers, founders, and tech enthusiasts.

## Mission Statement
The goal of Builders NYC is to bring NYC's tech startups directly to its customers through series' of curated events including hackathons, hack nights, demo nights, and much more. With Builders NYC, the spotlight is on the builders of NYC, the founders, the developers, and those with a passion for innovation.

## Core Concept
- Create tech-oriented community events designed to get people building with new and innovative tech built right here in NYC
- Create a place for those interested in tech to experiment, build, and one day be featured builders of NYC.

### Long-Term Vision
I would want Builders NYC to be a place where founders and startups can find their target audiences, work with them and learn from them. The events I have planned are designed with the goal of creating a stronger bridge between the consumer and the business through building with their specific tech and the applications of it. 

## Launch Plan
### First Event -- Community Hack Night (6/25)
- If sponsored by a startup
	- Begin first with inaugural presentation introducing Builders NYC, our mission and what we're all about.
	- Then introduce the featured startup of the night and what the venue will be building with tonight
	- Introduce the challenge for the night and begin the hack hour.
	- Demos, raffle prizes, and CTA for feedback at the end.
- If not sponsored by a startup
	- Begin first with inaugural presentation introducing Builders NYC, our mission and what we're all about.
	- Show off 4-6 different demos for our demo night while serving drinks and working the floor. 
	- raffle prizes, and CTA for feedback at the end.

## Goals for First Year
- Have at least 4 sponsored events, 8 in total
- Create a website for showcasing what builders have built in NYC at the community events.
- Host a full hackathon
